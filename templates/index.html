About Model Building Bye-Laws 2016

The Model Building Bye-Laws (MBBL) 2016 are guidelines issued by the Town & Country Planning Organisation (TCPO), Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, Government of India. They set out model norms for building design, construction, safety, and urban planning that states and local bodies can adopt or adapt.
